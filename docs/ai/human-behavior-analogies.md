# Human Behavior Analogies

This document explains human behavior analogies for maintainers. Operational authority remains in the master prompt and focused .ai rules. Use evidence, bounded work, progressive disclosure, and proof-based termination. ADHD and OCD are human clinical terms used here only as analogies for observable agent behaviors such as distraction, objective switching, repetitive checking, perfectionism, and perseveration. The framework does not diagnose AI systems or claim consciousness.
